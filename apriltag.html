Print an AprilTag

A printed tag inside the frame keeps the artwork in place when visitors look from the side. Visitors won't see it in AR, because the artwork covers it.

  1. Print at 100% / Actual size, not “fit to page”, preferably on matte paper (shiny paper reflects light into the camera). After printing, check the 50 mm bar with a ruler.
  2. Cut along the dashed line, keeping the white border around the black square.
  3. Fix it flat on the wall inside the frame opening, away from the edges, so the frame's lip doesn't hide it from the side. Bigger is better: about a third of the opening's width works well (for example 120–150 mm in a 40 × 60 cm frame). Two tags far apart make side views steadier still.
  4. Then either make a new setup with the tag in the photo, so the setup tool saves its exact position (most accurate), or simply use it: the viewer learns where the tag is the first time a phone recognises the frame from the front.
