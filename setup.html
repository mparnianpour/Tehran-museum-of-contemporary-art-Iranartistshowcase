Frame Setup Calibrate a new frame or wall for the AR slideshow
Open viewer →

Start with a photo of the frame on its wall.

1 Photo

Stand straight in front of the frame and photograph it with some wall around it, in the light the exhibition will have. The tracking works best when the frame and wall have visible texture or detail.

Optional: put printed AprilTags inside the frame or on the wall beside it before taking the photo. They keep the picture in place when visitors look from the side.

Edit an existing setup instead

2 Mark the opening

Drag the four handles onto the inner corners of the frame, where the picture should begin. A magnifier appears while you drag; arrow keys nudge the selected handle.

The handles are crossed. Place them top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left.

3 Size & tracking area

Inner size of the opening (any unit). Measuring gives the most accurate shape; leave it blank to estimate from the photo.

×

More surroundings give the tracker more detail to lock onto. If the wall behind the frame may change, keep the straightened image within the frame's outer edge, so only the frame is recognised.

4 Fit & look

Positive tucks the picture under the frame lip; negative leaves a reveal.

5 AprilTags (optional)

Printed tags keep the picture in place when visitors look from the side. They can go inside the frame, or on the wall beside it.

With the tag size and the size in step 3, tags anywhere and at any angle, on the panel, beside it, or on a pillar facing another way, are placed in 3D from the photo. The distance below is then not needed.

For a frame on a wall: measure from the wall to the edge of the opening. For a pillar: how far its front stands out from the panel, as a negative number. This keeps the picture aligned from the side.

For plain surfaces that image tracking can't recognise. Switched on automatically when step 6 rates tracking Poor. The tags must be in the photo.

6 Pillars and walls (optional)

If a pillar or the side wall of an alcove stands out in front of the picture, the viewer can let it hide the picture from the side, just as it hides the real surface. Use the same unit as the size in step 3.

"Covers the edge" is how much of the picture it hides when seen straight on; usually 0.

7 Compile tracking data

Analyses the straightened image so phones can recognise the frame. Takes 10–60 seconds.

8 Save & test

To publish it, unzip at the top of the site so the files land in setups/<id>/. Then set "setup": "<id>" in config.json, or link to index.html?setup=<id>.
